LitMatch

Reading & book discoveryHTML · CSS · JavaScript

A reading app with Russian and English texts. Browse the catalogue or discover a book, then return to your reading with bookmarks, saved progress and adjustable display settings.

Select a screen to take a closer look

3 images

Inside the app

A catalogue, categories, preference-based discovery, reading in two languages, a table of contents, bookmarks and text display settings.

How data is saved

Your profile, settings and reading position are stored in the current browser. There is no cross-device synchronisation.
